# Utah water: research fact sheet (supply, demand, places, law, GIS)

Compiled 2026-11-12 for the Utah chapter of the western-water map. Style follows `colorado.md`.

**Conventions**
- AF = acre-feet; KAF = thousand AF; MAF = million AF; M&I = municipal and industrial; DWRe = Utah Division of Water Resources; DWRi = Utah Division of Water Rights; GSL = Great Salt Lake; CUP = Central Utah Project; WY = water year (1 Oct to 30 Sep).
- Evidence tags: **[P]** = I opened the document itself and read the number. **[S]** = number reached me only through a search-engine summary; re-open before publishing. **[derived]** = my arithmetic. **unverified** = could not confirm.
- 2026 context: Utah's snowpack on 1 April 2026 was 22% of median, a record low. Do not present 2026 storage or flow as typical.

## 2. Great Salt Lake

Great Salt Lake Strike Team, "Great Salt Lake Data and Insights Summary", v2.0, 7 Jan 2026: https://d36oiwf74r1rap.cloudfront.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/GSL-Jan2026-Final.pdf [P, PDF text read]
- South arm ended WY2025 at 4,191.1 ft, "the third-lowest recorded elevation since 1903", in the "serious adverse effects" range.
- Healthy range begins at 4,198 ft (GSL Lake Level Matrix, 2013 Comprehensive Management Plan).
- "To reach the minimum healthy lake elevation (4,198 feet) by 2055, mean annual inflows would likely need to be ~2,465 KAF. Between 2000 and 2025, inflows averaged 1,665 KAF per year." The 800 KAF/yr scenario "represents the additional inflow needed to fill Great Salt Lake to the minimum healthy lake level (4,198 feet) by 2055."
- River inflow shares: Bear 64.4%, Jordan 20.7%, Weber 16.7% (as printed; they sum to 101.8%). Bear + Weber + Jordan streamflow fell below 1,000 KAF in 2021 and 2022, exceeded 2,000 KAF in 2023 and 2024, and was 1,281 KAF in 2025.
- Human depletions in the GSL basin, 2020-2024: agriculture 65.0%, M&I 26.8%, lake mineral extraction 5.7%, agricultural incidental loss 1.4%, reservoir evaporation 1.1%.
- "nearly 400,000 acre-feet of water being dedicated and delivered to Great Salt Lake between 2021 and 2025" (yearly: 18,386; 21,313; 149,277; 163,468; 45,385 preliminary).
- 2025: "Controlled releases of 80,000 acre-feet from Utah Lake"; Weber Basin Water Conservancy District "discharged over 100 KAF through the Willard Spur into Bear River Bay".
- Record low: "the lake's record low in November 2022". Watershed Enhancement Trust: H.B. 410 (2022) "endowed it with $40 million". S.B. 277 (2023) "$200 million for agriculture water optimization".
- Farmington Bay: "more than 120 square miles of the Farmington Bay lakebed became exposed, including 21 square miles of known dust hotspots".
- US Magnesium "filed for Chapter 11" (2025).
- Record low value 4,188.5 ft: state/USGS, repeated in the overview sheet.

Current level: USGS gauge 10010000 (Saltair, south arm) 4,190.0 ft on 12 Nov 2026; gauge 10010100 (Saline, north arm) 4,189.6 ft on 11 Nov 2026; south arm daily values 4,189.4 to 4,190.0 ft between 28 Sep and 9 Nov 2026 (provisional). ## 4. Conditions in 2026
- Western Water Assessment briefing, 9 Apr 2026 [P] https://wwa.colorado.edu/resources/intermountain-west-dashboard/briefing/april-9-2026-co-ut-wy : Utah snowpack 22% of median on 1 April, a record low; 60% of Utah SNOTEL sites melted out on 1 April (typically 2 of 179); Utah streamflow forecasts 20-55% of average; March 2026 was Utah's warmest on record by 5.5 F.

## 9. Figures I am least sure about
1. The Utah Water Budget numbers were read off a diagram image, not a table. They sum correctly (basins = 5,120,235; sectors = 5,120,235; depletion + return = 5,120,233).
2. NRCS 1 Nov 2026 storage is provisional data. "% full" is my division by the NRCS capacity, which can differ slightly from an operator's figure (Reclamation gives Strawberry 1,106,500 AF; NRCS 1,105,900).
3. Great Salt Lake 4,190.0 ft is a provisional USGS reading rounded to 0.1 ft.
4. Tunnel lengths for the Strawberry (about 3.7 mi) and Duchesne (about 6 mi) tunnels are measured from OpenStreetMap geometry, not taken from Reclamation.
5. The Fox 13 Lake Powell Pipeline article shows no date on the page. The card quotes the district manager without dating the statement.
6. US Magnesium plant coordinates (40.915, -112.733) and the Stratos site (Hansel Valley, 41.75, -112.65) are approximate and not from a surveyed source.
7. The 2025 hay study figures come through KSL's report, not the journal article.
